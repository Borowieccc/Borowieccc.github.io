About me

PB-00Profile

Hi, I'm Patryk.

I make games look, sound and feel the way they should.

I've worked in Unity since 2014. I started as a 2D artist and today I cover the whole visual side of a game: art direction, level art, materials, particles, UI and in-engine cutscenes. I also do audio, from sourcing SFX to composing music.

Most recently I was Art Director on Dark Stream, a psychological horror game by RedDeer.Games.

Outside of games I make posters, exhibit at poster fairs and have worked with Otodom. With Claude I do AI-assisted prototyping to build playable Unity prototypes on my own.
