About

Hi, I'm Patryk. I make games look, sound and feel the way they should.

I've been working in Unity since 2014. I started as a 2D artist doing UI/UX and 2D assets in many styles, from stylized to pixel art, and soon began building my own small games. Today I cover the whole visual side of a game: art direction, concept art, level art, textures, materials, particles and in-engine cutscenes. I also handle audio. I source and implement music and SFX, and I compose my own music; I've played synth and guitar since I was 11. Most recently I was Art Director on Dark Stream, a first-person psychological horror game by RedDeer.Games, where I owned the visuals, audio, UI/UX, the anomalies and the story integration.

Outside of games I'm a poster artist. I exhibit at poster fairs and have collaborated with Otodom. You can find my posters on Instagram.

I work with AI too. I use Claude every day for vibe coding: prototyping mechanics, tools and whole features in Unity, which lets me take ideas from art to playable on my own.

Featured · Art Director

Art Director RedDeer.Games PC · Steam Full release March 2027 5-person team

Dark Stream

A first-person psychological horror about catching anomalies. You play a disgraced content creator trying to win back an audience by livestreaming a night in a haunted house, while the Twitch chat shapes what happens. The reveal trailer premiered on IGN, and a free demo is out now on Steam.

Made by a core team of five: a programmer, a game designer, a narrative designer, a tester and me as the only artist. Besides the art, I also took care of audio, UI/UX and cutscenes.

What I did:

  • Art direction: the game's whole visual direction, look and mood.
  • Anomalies: designed and built the anomalies, the core of the gameplay.
  • Story integration: tied all narrative elements together in the game.
  • Cutscenes: created every cutscene in Unity.
  • UI & UX: the complete interface and player experience.
  • Audio: the entire audio side, music and SFX.
  • Assets: sourced 3D models and reworked their textures and materials to fit the style.

Personal projects

Füel

Co-op · in development · 2026 Unity with Maciek Sozanski

A co-op game for up to four players, built on FishNet. You escort a huge mechanical spider through a foggy desert junkyard and keep it running on the trash you pick up along the way, climbing hand over hand with a separate stamina for each hand. The game was inspired by co-op friendslop games like PEAK, and by indie titles with great atmosphere and mechanics, like White Knuckle and Rain World.

My part: the whole visual side of the game, level design, level art, UX and UI, working with Claude along the way. Code, game design and tech art by Maciek Sozanski.

What a show!

Idle clicker · prototype Unity Claude

A Unity prototype of an idle clicker where you run a TV station broadcasting a war live. You're the director, and your job is to keep people watching: switch between cameras, jump into a random soldier's bodycam, trigger events like the Wall of Death, and deal with chat challenges and signal failures while the battle plays out.

A sponsor system lets you run cheesy 80s-style ads to earn gold and temporarily boost your warriors. But viewers hate commercials: every ad break costs you audience and slows the ratings bar, so you have to pick your moments.
