Personal projects

Games and prototypes I make after hours.

PP-01Co-op · in development · 2026 · Unity · with Maciek Sozanski

Füel

A co-op game for up to four players, built on FishNet. You escort a huge mechanical spider through a foggy desert junkyard, keep it running on the trash you pick up, and climb hand over hand with a separate stamina for each hand.

My part

The whole visual side of the game, level design, level art, UX and UI, working with Claude along the way. Code, game design and tech art by Maciek Sozanski.

PP-02Idle clicker · prototype · Unity · made with Claude

What a show!

An idle clicker prototype where you run a TV station broadcasting a war live. As the director you switch cameras, jump into a soldier's bodycam, trigger events and handle chat challenges to keep people watching. Cheesy 80s ads earn gold and boost your warriors, but every ad break costs you viewers.
